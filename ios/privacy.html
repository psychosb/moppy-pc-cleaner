Privacy Policy — Moppy - Photo Cleaner

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Moppy is an app by Lumat Studio for iPhone and iPad that helps free up space in your photo library and tidy up contacts.

What Moppy collects

Nothing. Moppy does not collect, store off-device or transmit any personal data. The app has no server of its own, requires no account, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

What Moppy accesses on the device

  • Photos and videos: to find duplicates, similar and blurry shots, screenshots, large videos and Live Photos, and to compress videos or convert Live Photos when you ask. Analysis (including text recognition in screenshots) happens entirely on the device, using Apple's own technologies. No image ever leaves your iPhone.
  • Contacts: to find duplicate or incomplete contacts and, once you confirm, merge or delete them. Nothing is copied out of your address book.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security.

What is stored on the device

To keep scans fast, Moppy keeps on the device (in the app's private area) numeric fingerprints of your photos, the text recognized in screenshots you chose to read, the list of items you chose to keep and a history of cleanups. This data is removed with the app, or in Settings → Clear cache.

Purchases

Purchases (Moppy Premium yearly or lifetime) are processed by Apple through the App Store. Moppy never sees or stores payment data. Purchase verification happens on the device through Apple's StoreKit.

Deleted items

Everything Moppy deletes goes to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where it stays for 30 days and can be recovered. Merged or deleted contacts cannot be recovered by the app.

Children

Moppy is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
